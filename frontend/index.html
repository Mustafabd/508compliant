Why compliance teams keep this around

508/WCAG backlogs are expensive to clear by hand

Manually tagging headings, writing alt text, and fixing reading order for hundreds of legacy PDFs is slow, repetitive work. This automates the bulk of it in seconds per document.

ADA/508 exposure is a real legal risk

Untagged, inaccessible PDFs on a public or internal site are a common target for accessibility complaints and lawsuits. Closing the gap on your existing library reduces that exposure.

Procurement and grants often require it

Government contracts, higher-ed material, and many grant-funded programs require documents to meet Section 508/WCAG 2.1 AA — this gets a document most of the way there before a final review.

You get a report, not just a promise

Every conversion returns a checklist of what was fixed automatically and what still needs a human — no false sense of "certified compliant" on something no one reviewed.

How it works

  1. Upload a PDF. Drag and drop, up to 25 MB and 300 pages.
  2. We tag it. Headings, paragraphs, and figures are rewritten into a real PDF structure tree with marked content — not just a "tagged" flag. Images get alt text, and title/language metadata is set.
  3. Download and review. Get the remediated PDF plus a report of what was automated and what still needs a manual check — reading order on complex layouts, table headers, color contrast, and form labels aren't automatically verified.

What this tool doesn't do

Reading order and heading levels are inferred heuristically (font size and content order) — they aren't a guarantee of semantic correctness for complex or multi-column layouts. Color contrast, table row/column headers, form field labels, and document outline/bookmarks are not automatically checked. Treat every conversion as a strong head start, and review the report before publishing anything you're relying on for compliance.

508Compliant automates what can be reliably automated in PDF accessibility remediation. It is not a substitute for a manual accessibility audit.
